# JE2 — 3D Crown-Volume Opportunity on a Balanced Living Frontier

Status: experimental morphology checkpoint on top of JE1. Do not merge to `main`.

## Why JE2 exists

JE1 proved that mature scaffold-lineage reachability was a real missing prerequisite:

- every accepted first-order scaffold remained represented through 3k/10k/30k across the measured souls;
- the selected continuation frontier stayed only 16–24 living tips;
- runtime, LOD budgets, persisted depth, depth-separated projected crossings, and XYZ clearance all remained healthy.

But the browser crown remained hollow. At 30k it became a cleaner, more balanced perimeter arch / wreath rather than occupying the crown interior.

Rejected JD had already tested a deterministic 3D volume-opportunity signal, but JD ran on a frontier where entire accepted scaffold lineages could already be developmentally unreachable. JE2 repeats **only that opportunity experiment** on top of JE1's balanced living frontier.

## Hypothesis

A 3D opportunity signal can only improve mature crown occupancy if multiple established scaffold systems still have living meristems capable of responding to under-served volume.

JE2 therefore changes no reachability, geometry-generation, depth, renewal, envelope, or angle rule. It changes only the mature opportunity term in candidate scoring.

## Exact scoring experiment

Through entry 1,000:

- use the existing `crownGapScore` exactly as before;
- preserve the accepted young tree exactly;
- restDepth remains zero.

After entry 1,000:

- replace the projected-XY `crownGapScore` term with JD's tested deterministic 3D attraction-field opportunity score;
- 32 soul-derived attraction points occupy normalized interior crown volume;
- points already colonized within the fixed kill radius are ignored;
- a candidate earns opportunity only when its endpoint reduces distance to currently under-served attraction points;
- only the strongest four improvements contribute;
- score remains bounded at 1.05.

This is a competition signal, not a target-forcing vector. Candidate headings, length, depth delta, tropism, and legal XYZ clearance are unchanged.

## Locked from JE1

JE2 must not change:

- <=1k topology, XY geometry, or zero-depth behavior;
- schema V3 / persisted restDepth experiment;
- JE0 sympodial successor semantics;
- JE1 four-meristem-per-scaffold minimum reserve;
- mature 64-birth recent frontier and 48-birth side-growth recency;
- renewal eligibility/divergence;
- candidate heading samples;
- branch angle, curvature, tropism, or light targets;
- depth tendency/magnitude;
- mature XY or Z envelopes;
- XYZ collision clearance;
- structural cadence;
- foliage attachment, leaf form, LOD grouping, seasons, motion, or UI.

## Acceptance

JE2 is useful only if all of these agree:

1. accepted scaffold coverage remains 4/4 in the measured frontier checkpoints and selected tip count remains bounded;
2. mature depth remains bounded and all projected crossings remain safely separated in XYZ;
3. unsafe spatial pairs remain zero;
4. 30k longevity/runtime and existing LOD budgets pass unchanged;
5. the 10k and 30k browser crown materially occupies more interior volume / projected centre space than JE1;
6. the tree still reads as the same asymmetrical organism, not a spherical fuzz, central knot, symmetric lollipop, or random spaghetti.

If JE2 keeps the crown hollow despite balanced living lineages and a genuine 3D opportunity signal, stop adding opportunity coefficients. The next diagnosis should inspect **candidate direction generation and reachable volume**: whether the available headings/depth deltas can physically reach the attraction field under the mature envelopes.
