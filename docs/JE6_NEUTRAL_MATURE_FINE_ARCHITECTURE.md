# JE6 — Neutral Mature Fine-Continuation Architecture

Status: experimental morphology checkpoint on top of JE5. Do not merge to `main`.

## Evidence from JE5

JE5 decomposed the exact production score for real mature decisions and showed that the old axis-length architecture term is the dominant suppressor of interior-reaching candidates.

At 30k, highest-opportunity candidates are commonly established order-3 continuations (~10–12 modules) carrying architecture contributions around -1.75 to -2.4, while winners are often short/new order-4 axes with architecture around 0 to +0.6.

A behavior-free hypothetical that neutralized order-3/4 continuation architecture after 1k caused the highest-opportunity candidate to overtake the historical winner in 23/48 sampled 10k+30k decisions (~48%). That is large enough to justify a generated-tree experiment, but not large enough to assume success.

## JE6 single change

After entry 1,000 only:

- if the candidate relation is `continuation` and order is 3 or 4, `architectureScore` contributes **0**;
- the young <=1k architecture rule remains byte-for-byte unchanged;
- order-0/1/2 continuation architecture remains unchanged;
- lateral and renewal architecture remains unchanged.

This removes both juvenile fine-axis forces from mature continuation competition:

- no positive establishment bonus for a short/new fine continuation;
- no accumulating excess-length penalty for a long established fine continuation.

It does **not** remove architecture from renewal/lateral formation or from major branch hierarchy.

## Acceptance order

### Gate 1 — exact technical contracts
Without loosening limits:
- full tests/build;
- <=1k 128-soul gate;
- 30k longevity/runtime and LOD budgets;
- deterministic history/append invariants.

### Gate 2 — mature reachability/spatial safety
Across the measured 3k/10k/30k souls:
- accepted scaffold lineages remain reachable;
- frontier remains bounded;
- mature depth remains bounded;
- projected crossings remain depth-separated;
- unsafe XYZ pairs remain zero;
- minimum non-local XYZ clearance remains safe.

### Gate 3 — browser morphology
The generated 10k/30k crown must materially improve over JE4's hollow perimeter/roof:
- more persistent structural occupation of projected interior/centre space;
- major scaffold hierarchy still readable;
- no switch to a central knot, ball, bush, chaotic web, or repetitive zig-zag;
- same organism should still read continuously from 3k -> 10k -> 30k.

If JE6 improves decision composition but mature morphology remains hollow, do not add more architecture exceptions in the same branch. Re-measure the generated JE6 candidate/score surface and identify the next limiting mechanism.
