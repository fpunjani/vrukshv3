# JE1 — Scaffold-Balanced Mature Meristem Reserve

Status: experimental continuation-frontier checkpoint on top of JE0. Do not merge to `main`.

## Evidence from corrected JE0

JE0 restored sympodial continuation/renewal successor semantics inside the 2.5D JC base and remeasured *living* terminal meristems correctly.

The global 64-structural-birth mature recency window still causes lineage loss / monopolization:

- ash-01 retains 4/4 accepted first-order scaffolds through 30k, but one scaffold holds ~43% of the eligible frontier;
- ash-02 has only 3/4 represented scaffolds and one scaffold holds ~90% of eligible tips by 30k;
- ash-03 falls from 4/4 at 3k to 3/4 at 10k to 2/4 at 30k, with one scaffold holding ~92% of eligible tips;
- ash-04 retains 4/4 but one scaffold holds ~62% at 30k.

JE0 spatial diagnostics remain healthy: all measured post-1k modules use persisted depth, projected crossings are depth-separated, unsafe XYZ pairs remain zero, and mature extent/runtime remain bounded.

Therefore the next intervention belongs to **frontier reachability**, not depth magnitude, attraction fields, renewal angle, or crown envelopes.

## JE1 hypothesis

Keep the existing global recent frontier, but prevent an accepted scaffold lineage from becoming developmentally unreachable solely because other lineages won more recent structural events.

Historical wood remains permanent. This reserve does not make old interior buds active; it applies only to genuine living terminal meristems (modules with no continuation-or-renewal successor).

## Selection policy

After entry 1,000:

1. start with the exact JE0 non-trunk continuation frontier: every living terminal tip whose axis was active within the last 64 structural births;
2. resolve each living terminal tip to the accepted first-order scaffold (`order=1`, lateral, born <=1,000) in its ancestry;
3. for each accepted scaffold with fewer than **4** recent living tips, add its newest dormant living tips until that lineage has four selected tips or runs out of living tips;
4. do not remove recent tips from dominant scaffolds in this first experiment;
5. trunk continuation keeps JE0's existing 64-birth treatment.

This is a minimum-meristem reserve, not a fairness score. Candidate scoring is unchanged.

For the measured JE0 souls this should keep the non-trunk continuation pool roughly in the existing tens-of-tips range rather than growing with lifetime history.

## Locked layers

JE1 must not change:

- <=1k tree/topology/XY/depth-zero behavior;
- schema V3 / persisted restDepth experimental contract;
- sympodial renewal semantics from JE0;
- 48-structural-birth mature side-growth window;
- renewal eligibility/angle;
- candidate headings/tropism/light targets;
- XY/Z mature envelopes;
- XYZ collision clearance;
- `crownGapScore` / opportunity score;
- structural cadence;
- foliage, leaf form, LOD grouping, seasons, or UI.

## Acceptance questions

1. Does every accepted first-order scaffold retain a bounded living continuation reserve through 3k/10k/30k?
2. Does the largest-scaffold share fall materially in previously collapsed souls without forcing perfect symmetry?
3. Does the total selected continuation frontier remain bounded and operationally cheap?
4. Do normal 30k runtime, LOD budgets, XYZ safety, and hierarchy remain green?
5. Most importantly: does the browser crown regain interior continuity instead of a U/ring/perimeter, without becoming a symmetric candelabra or dense central knot?

If lineage reachability improves numerically but the visual remains hollow, then frontier extinction was real but not sufficient. Only then should 3D opportunity competition be reconsidered on top of the balanced frontier.
