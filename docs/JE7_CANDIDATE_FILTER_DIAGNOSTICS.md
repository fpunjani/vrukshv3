# JE7 — Mature Candidate-Filter Diagnostics

Status: diagnostics-only checkpoint. It must not be merged as a morphology policy.

## Why this exists

JE6 proved that neutralizing juvenile fine-axis architecture changes mature score composition, but it did not repair the 10k/30k crown. The generated late-life decision surface still often has no legal candidate with positive mature-volume opportunity.

The next question is not which score term should be changed. It is where the offered proposals disappear:

- mature volume envelope;
- spatial clearance;
- below-ground/non-finite validity;
- or another legality filter.

## What JE7 adds

JE7 exposes rejection counts from the exact production candidate-generation and legality path used by `growStructuralEvent`, but only when the observational diagnostic helper runs. Production growth behavior is unchanged.

For mature-volume failures it separates horizontal, vertical, and depth bounds, and distinguishes ordinary continuation proposals from the bounded opportunity-steered proposals added by JE4. Spatial-clearance failures are also split by proposal source.

The measurement test is opt-in through `VITE_JE7_CANDIDATE_FILTER_MEASURE=1` and samples the same 3k/10k/30k checkpoints and four deterministic souls used by the mature audit.

## Current evidence

On six sampled 30k decisions for ash-01, the local JE7 probe observed:

- 28 uncolonized attractors on average;
- 0 improvable attractors;
- 0 candidates with positive opportunity;
- 5.7 legal candidates on average;
- 584 mature-volume rejections, including 406 vertical-bound failures;
- 152 spatial-clearance rejections;
- no depth-bound failures.

Both ordinary and steered proposals are affected by the vertical bound. This is evidence about the candidate filter, not yet a justification for widening the envelope.

## Locked

JE7 must not change:

- accepted <=1k topology or geometry;
- candidate headings, depth samples, frontier policy, or score weights;
- foliage, LOD, seasons, motion, or UI;
- persistence/schema semantics.

The next morphology experiment may only begin after the multi-soul JE7 measurement is reviewed and identifies one narrow legality mechanism to test.
